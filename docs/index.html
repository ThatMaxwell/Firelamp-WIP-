Firelamp OS · Arch Linux · Coming soon

Your computer, now with a second cursor.

Firelamp is a Linux desktop built so an AI can use it the way you do. It sees every window, moves its own cursor, and asks before anything you can't undo.

Follow the build ↗

Most AI agents use your computer through screenshots. They take a picture, guess what it means, click, and take another. Firelamp doesn't guess. The OS tells the AI exactly what's on screen, the moment it changes.

01

It reads the screen.

Every window, button and text field reaches the AI already labeled, with its exact position. Nothing to decode, nothing to guess.

02

It shows you the plan first.

Before it touches anything, you see every step it's about to take. Say go, or change the plan.

03

It has its own cursor.

The fire cursor moves, clicks and types right next to yours. Keep working, or sit back and watch.

04

It asks first.

Deleting files, sending messages, paying for things. The OS itself stops and asks you, every single time.

05

You can stop it. Instantly.

Reach into the window it's working in and it pauses itself. Press Esc and it stops.

Try it on the fire cursor wandering around this page.

It's running.

06

It shows its work.

Everything it did lands in a timeline you can actually read: what, where, and why.

A brain, and reflexes.

The brain

An LLM you talk to, by voice or text. It understands what you want and plans it out, step by step.

The reflexes
