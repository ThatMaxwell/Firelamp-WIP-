Firelamp OS · Arch Linux · Out now

Your computer, now with a second cursor.

Firelamp is a Linux desktop built so an AI can use it the way you do. It sees every window, moves its own cursor, and asks before anything you can't undo.

Follow the build ↗

Most AI agents use your computer through screenshots. They take a picture, guess what it means, click, and take another. Firelamp doesn't guess. The OS tells the AI exactly what's on screen, the moment it changes.
